Coconut Rice

Description

This recipe is the Nigerian version of cocunut rice which is slightly spicy and savory. This recipe is not for beginners as it lacks measurement and timing. This recipe is from chefolaskitchen.com.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Coconut Milk ( Canned or Fresh )
  • Chicken or Vegetable Stock
  • Onions
  • Bell Pepper
  • Coconut Oil
  • Shrimp
  • Habanero
  • Seasongins ( salt, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, curry, and thyme )
  • Crayfish& #40 optional )

Steps

  1. Prepare your stock See how for chicken stock
  2. Blend bell pepper with habanero
  3. Sauté onions in coconut oil until translucent
  4. Add blended pepper mix and crayfish
  5. Pour in coconut milk and stock
  6. Wash your rice and add it
  7. Cook on low heat for 20 minutes
  8. Clean and season your shrimp with paprika, garlic powder, onion powder and salt
  9. Fry Shrimp in coconut oil until cooked
  10. Stir fry diced bell pepper until slightly tender
  11. Add fried bell pepper and shrimp into cooked rice
  12. Mix well and enjoy
