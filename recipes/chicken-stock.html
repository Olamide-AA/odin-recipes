Chicken Stock

Description

This recipe shows you how to create a rich chicken stock. This stock can be used for other recipes. This recipe is not for beginners as it lacks measurement and timing.This recipe is from chefolaskitchen.com.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Onions
  • Scotch Bonnet
  • Ginger
    • a
  • Garlic
  • Thyme
  • Bay Leave
  • Seasoning ( salt, curry, bouillon )

Steps

  1. Clean chicken thoroughly
  2. Season with the rest of the ingredients
  3. Add water
  4. Cook until tender
  5. Seprate chicken from stock
