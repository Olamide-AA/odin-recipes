Egg Tuna Sandwich

Description

This recipe is easy and convenient for when you want a filling and tasty meal without doing too much work. This recipe is not for beginners as it lacks measurement and timing.

Ingredients

  • Egg
  • Tuna
  • Mayonaise
  • Bread

Steps

  1. Hard boil the egg
  2. Cut the egg into cubes
  3. Add tuna to the egg
  4. Add mayonaise
  5. Mix together
  6. Spread the mixture on the bread
